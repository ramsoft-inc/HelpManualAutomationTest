# Table of Worklist Fields

The following table describes the columns/fields available in the
worklist:

|**Column/Field**|**Description**|
| :- | :- |
|**# of Frames (Frame Count)**|<p>- Indicates the total number of image frames available within the OmegaAI image viewer for a selected image or series.</p><p>- The frame count is fetched directly from the image data loaded into the OmegaAI viewer and reflects the frames rendered from the underlying DICOM instance(s).</p><p>- Focuses on how many visual frames the user can navigate in the viewer.</p><p>- This value represents how many individual frames can be scrolled through or played sequentially in the viewer (for example, axial slices or multi-frame images).</p><p>- If a study does not contain multi-frame or stack-based image data, this field may appear blank.</p><p></p>|
|**# of Images (Image Count)**|<p>- Indicates the total number of distinct images contained within a study. </p><p>- This count is derived from the DICOM images associated with the study, regardless of how many frames each image contains.</p><p>- Focuses on how many image objects make up the study, not how they are displayed in the viewer.</p><p>- Each image typically corresponds to a DICOM image object or instance.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**# of Instances (Instance Count)**|<p>- Refers to the total number of DICOM instances (objects) associated with a study.</p><p>- This count is derived directly from the study’s DICOM dataset at the storage/object level.</p><p>- Focuses on how many DICOM objects exist for the study.</p><p>- Each instance is a stored DICOM object, which may be single-frame or multi-frame.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**# of Reports (Number of Associated Reports)**|<p>- Displays the total number of reports associated with the selected study. </p><p>- This count is fetched from the study’s linked report records for the corresponding accession, including preliminary, final, and addendum reports available in the system.</p>|
|<p>**# of Documents**</p><p></p>|<p>- Represents the total number of documents associated with the study or order.</p><p>- Automatically calculated based on the documents linked to the study, such as reports, attachments, or related files stored in the system.</p><p>- Displayed as a numeric value and can be used as a filter to identify studies with or without associated documentation.</p><p>- Used to track document availability and support workflow tasks such as review, verification, and completion.</p>|
|**Accession # (Accession Number)**|<p>- Unique identifier assigned to an imaging study or order when it is created in the RIS or HIS.</p><p>- Fetched into the worklist through system integrations (for example, HL7 or DICOM).</p><p>- Used to link the study across RIS, PACS, reporting, and billing systems.</p><p>- Represents a single imaging request and remains consistent across workflow steps.</p><p>- Enables accurate study identification, tracking, and retrieval.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Assigning Authority (Issuer)**|<p>- Identifies the issuer, representing the organization or authority that issued the identifier.</p><p>- Defaults to the organization’s configured issuer when no external issuer is specified.</p><p>- Supports accurate identification and interoperability in multi-organization environments.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Account #**|<p>- A unique identifier assigned to a patient account within the system.</p><p>- Automatically generated by the system or sourced from the upstream registration/ADT system.</p><p>- Used to group and track all studies, visits, and billing records associated with the patient.</p><p>- Helps distinguish patients with similar demographics and reduces the risk of record duplication.</p><p>- Commonly used in search filters, reconciliation, reporting, and financial workflows.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Account Status**|<p>- Indicates the current status of the patient’s account in the system.</p><p>- Derived from the patient or account configuration maintained by the organization.</p><p>- Used to control visibility, workflow eligibility, and operational handling of studies.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Age at Study**|<p>- Represents the patient’s age at the time the study was performed.</p><p>- Calculated using the patient’s date of birth and the study date.</p><p>- Reflects age at the moment of imaging, not the current age.</p><p>- Used for clinical context, interpretation, and reporting accuracy.</p><p>- Important for age-dependent protocols, measurements, and assessments.</p>|
|<p>**Anatomic Focus**</p><p>**(Target Anatomy)**</p>|<p>- Indicates the specific anatomical structure or organ system examined in the study.</p><p>- Fetched from the study metadata provided by the originating order or imaging system (RIS/PACS).</p><p>- Used to provide clinical context and support study filtering, categorization, and review.</p><p>- Identifies a specific organ or targeted anatomical region (for example, Abdomen Wall, Brain, Liver, Pancreas, Aorta, or Knee).</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Availability**|<p>- Represents the availability status of the study or associated resources.</p><p>- Derived from system workflow states and object availability.</p><p>- Used to determine whether the study is ready for viewing or further processing.</p>|
|**Authorization Period**|<p>- Represents the valid date range for an insurance authorization.</p><p>- Captured from authorization details entered manually or received from integrated systems.</p><p>- Used to verify that the study is scheduled and performed within the approved timeframe.</p>|
|**Appointment Cancellation Reason**|<p>- Indicates the reason an appointment was cancelled.</p><p>- Sourced from appointment management records at the time of cancellation.</p><p>- Captures standardized or configured cancellation reasons recorded in the system.</p><p></p>|
|**AE Title**|<p>- The Application Entity (AE) Title identifying the source or destination DICOM device associated with the study.</p><p>- Captured automatically from the DICOM metadata sent by the imaging modality or source device.</p><p>- Displays the configured AE Title assigned to the sending or receiving system. </p><p>- Used to identify and route studies between modalities, PACS, and integrated systems.</p><p>- Supports device identification, workflow routing, and system-level filtering.</p><p>- Helps trace the origin of the study for operational and troubleshooting purposes.</p>|
|**Birth Sex**|<p>- The gender assigned to the patient at birth.</p><p>- Captured during patient registration as part of demographic details.</p><p>- Used for patient identification, clinical context, and reporting.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Body Part**|<p>- Identifies the general body region examined or intended to be examined as part of a study (for example, Chest, Abdomen, Eye).</p><p>- Fetched from the study order or procedure metadata, based on the body part selected during order entry or protocol configuration.</p><p>- Applicable across different stages of a study lifecycle.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**CDS ID (Clinical Decision Support Identifier)**|<p>- Unique identifier associated with a Clinical Decision Support (CDS) rule or configuration.</p><p>- Derived from the CDS configuration defined within the system.</p><p>- Used to filter, reference, or track records influenced by CDS logic.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Clinical Comments**|<p>- Contains clinician-entered notes related to the study or patient encounter.</p><p>- Sourced from the document viewer (clinician’s notes) entered during order creation or review.</p><p>- May include observations, provisional diagnoses, or relevant remarks.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Custom Field 1**|<p>- A configurable field used to capture additional, site-specific information.</p><p>- Values are populated based on how the field is defined within the system configuration.</p><p>- The content and usage depend on local configuration and workflow requirements.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Custom Field 2**|<p>- A configurable field used to capture additional, site-specific information.</p><p>- Data is populated based on how the field is defined and used within the system setup.</p><p>- The purpose and values of this field are determined by local configuration and operational workflows.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Communication Status Name**|<p>- Represents the communication status associated with the study or report.</p><p>- Captured from communication or notification workflows.</p><p>- Used to track whether results or updates have been communicated.</p>|
|<p>**Consulting Physician**</p><p></p>|<p>- Represents the physician consulted for the study or patient care.</p><p>- Captured from order details or manual assignment.</p><p>- Used for clinical coordination and reporting.</p>|
|**Department**|<p>- Identifies the department to which the study is assigned.</p><p>- Values are populated from the department master data configured in the system.</p><p>- Enables filtering of studies based on departmental ownership for operational, reporting, and workload views.</p><p>- The department association is typically derived from the ordering context, scheduled location, or managing organization rules, depending on workflow configuration.</p><p>- Supports type-ahead search and selection, displaying matching departments as the user enters text.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Date/Time Addendum**|<p>- Displays the date and time when an addendum was created.</p><p>- Populated from the addendum metadata at the time the addendum is saved.</p><p>- Represents the addendum creation timestamp, independent of the original study or report date.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Date/Time Last Updated**|<p>- Displays the most recent date and time when the study record was modified.</p><p>- The value is system-generated and fetched from the study’s audit/update timestamp within the backend data store.</p><p>- Used for filtering and sorting studies based on recent activity, regardless of current study status (ordered, in progress, completed, or rejected).</p><p>- Reflects updates resulting from actions such as status changes, data edits, or workflow progress.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Date/Time Ordered**|<p>- Indicates the date and time when the study order was created in the system.</p><p>- Captured at the point the order is placed in the system.</p><p>- Can be used for order lifecycle tracking and TAT analysis.</p><p>- Date range selection is mandatory to activate time-based filtering.</p><p>- Time selection becomes available only after a valid date range is selected.</p><p>- The timestamp reflects the system/server time at which the order was created.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Date/Time Read**|<p>- Indicates the date and time when the study was marked as read by a radiologist.</p><p>- The value is fetched from the study reporting or interpretation record.</p><p>- Can be used for report TAT measurements.</p><p>- Captured when the study status transitions to Read.</p><p>- Users must first select a date range to activate time selection.</p><p>- Time values refine results within the selected date window only.</p><p>- Supports preset ranges (e.g., Today, Last 7 Days) as well as custom ranges.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Date/Time Received**|<p>- Indicates the date and time when the RIS/PACS received the study.</p><p>- Populated when the study is successfully ingested via DICOM/HL7 interfaces.</p><p>- Used for analysis of study intake and processing timelines.</p><p>- Reflects the system/server time of receipt.</p><p>- Time filtering is enabled only after selecting a specific date range.</p><p>- Supports preset ranges (e.g., Today, Last 7 Days) as well as custom ranges.</p><p>&emsp;</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Date/Time Signed**|<p>- Represents the date and time when the report was electronically signed.</p><p>- Recorded at the moment of final report sign-off.</p><p>- Supports report completion tracking and compliance auditing.</p><p>- The timestamp is sourced from the report signature audit trail.</p><p>- Date selection is required before the time picker is enabled.</p><p>- Time filters apply only to studies within the selected date range.</p><p>- Supports preset ranges (e.g., Today, Last 7 Days) as well as custom ranges.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Date/Time Transcribed**|<p>- Indicates the date and time when the transcription was completed.</p><p>- Captured after manual transcription or speech-to-text processing finishes.</p><p>- Used to track transcription turnaround times.</p><p>- Timestamp is sourced from the transcription or dictation workflow.</p><p>- Users must select a date range to enable time-based filtering.</p><p>- Time selection refines transcription events within the chosen date range.</p><p>- Supports preset ranges (e.g., Today, Last 7 Days) as well as custom ranges.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Date/Time Verified**|<p>- Indicates the date and time when the study was verified.</p><p>- Automatically populated when the study status changes to verified.</p><p>- Used for workflow completion tracking and audit purposes.</p><p>- Timestamp is sourced from the workflow status log.</p><p>- Time selection remains disabled until a date range is selected.</p><p>- Supports preset ranges (e.g., Today, Last 7 Days) as well as custom ranges.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Date of Birth**|<p>- The patient’s date of birth captured during patient registration.</p><p>- Fetched from the patient demographic record</p><p>- Used for patient identification, age calculation, and clinical context.</p>|
|**Eligibility**|<p>- Indicates the patient’s insurance coverage eligibility for the associated study.</p><p>- Values are derived from insurance verification workflows or eligibility checks performed against payer information.</p><p>- Used to support billing readiness, workflow prioritization, and operational filtering of studies.</p><p>- Possible statuses include Yes, No, Pending, and Unknown, reflecting the current verification outcome.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Ethnicity**|<p>- Represents the patient’s self-reported ethnicity captured during registration.</p><p>- Values are sourced from the demographic record and follow configured ethnicity lists.</p><p>- Used for demographic reporting, analytics, and regulatory or compliance purposes.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|<p>**Filler Order #**</p><p>**(Filler Order Number)**</p>|<p>- A unique order number assigned by the receiving application (filler system).</p><p>- Retrieved from the receiving application at order creation.</p><p>- Serves as the permanent identifier for the order and all related observations.</p><p>- Used to consistently reference and track the order throughout its lifecycle.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Financial Class**|<p>- Indicates the patient’s payment or reimbursement category for the study or encounter.</p><p>- Values are derived from the patient’s registration and billing information configured in the system.</p><p>- Used for financial workflows such as billing, reporting, eligibility checks, and revenue analysis.</p><p>- Classifies how charges are expected to be covered (for example, insurance-based, government-funded, or self-funded).</p><p>- Supports filtering and segmentation of studies based on payment responsibility.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Gender**|<p>- Represents the gender as declared or recorded for the patient.</p><p>- Values are captured during patient registration or demographic updates.</p><p>- Used for demographic reference, reporting, and downstream clinical or administrative workflows.</p><p>- May include standard options such as Female, Male, Other, or Unknown, depending on system configuration.</p><p>- Supports filtering and grouping of studies based on patient demographic attributes.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Healthcare Service**|<p>- Identifies the healthcare service or operational location where the study is scheduled to be performed.</p><p>- Values are sourced from configured healthcare services/resources within the system (for example, modality and location master data).</p><p>- Used to filter, route, and prioritize studies based on service location or modality availability.</p><p>- Typically represents a modality, room, mobile unit, or service area (for example, CT Room, Mobile CT, or a named clinical service).</p><p>- Assigned at the time of scheduling and may be updated if the study is rescheduled or reassigned.</p><p>- Supports operational reporting, workload distribution, and resource utilization analysis.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**History**|<p>- Captures a summary of the patient’s relevant medical history, symptoms, or presenting complaint related to the study.</p><p>- Typically entered by the ordering provider or clinical staff at the time of order creation or scheduling.</p><p>- Used by radiologists and technologists to understand clinical context and support accurate interpretation.</p><p>- May include prior conditions, current symptoms, injury details, or clinical indications that justify the exam.</p><p>- Sourced from the order entry workflow, referral information, or inbound HL7 order messages (when integrated).</p><p>- Supports filtering and review workflows where clinical indication or history is required for triage or quality checks.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Home Phone**|<p>- Stores the patient’s primary home telephone number.</p><p>- Typically captured during patient registration or demographic intake.</p><p>- Used for non-urgent patient communication, appointment coordination, or administrative follow-ups.</p><p>- Sourced from the patient demographics record within the system or received via inbound ADT/registration messages (when integrated).</p><p>- May be used as a searchable or filterable field where patient contact information is required.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Imaging Organization**|<p>- Identifies the organization responsible for performing the imaging study.</p><p>- Values are populated from configured imaging organizations within the system and may include externally integrated organizations.</p><p>- Used to filter, group, and report studies based on the performing imaging organization.</p><p>- Represents the imaging provider or facility under which the study is conducted (for example, hospital networks, imaging groups, or internal organizations).</p><p>- Supports organizations with long or descriptive names, including those exceeding standard character lengths.</p><p>- Commonly leveraged in multi-organization or multi-site environments to distinguish studies performed across different providers.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Insurance Copay**|<p>- Indicates the portion of the total cost that the patient is required to pay toward the study.</p><p>- Retrieved from the patient’s insurance and billing details associated with the study.</p><p>- Used to support billing review, financial validation, and eligibility-related workflows.</p><p>- Can be defined as either a fixed monetary amount (currency) or a percentage of the total charge.</p><p>- May vary based on the patient’s insurance plan, financial class, and coverage rules.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Insurance Expiry**|<p>- Indicates the date on which the patient’s insurance coverage is no longer valid.</p><p>- Retrieved from the insurance details recorded in the patient’s demographic.</p><p>- Used to determine coverage validity at the time of scheduling, ordering, or performing a study.</p><p>- Helps identify studies that may require insurance renewal, re-verification, or self-pay handling.</p><p>- Supports filtering and reporting based on active versus expired insurance coverage.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Insurance Status**|<p>- Indicates the current state of the patient’s insurance coverage (for example, Eligible, Pending, Ineligible, or Unknown).</p><p>- Derived from insurance eligibility and verification workflows within the system.</p><p>- Used to support billing decisions, scheduling readiness, and operational follow-up.</p><p>- Reflects the most recent insurance verification result associated with the patient or study.</p><p>- Enables filtering and reporting based on insurance verification outcomes.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Imaging Modality**|<p>- The imaging technique or equipment used to perform the study.</p><p>- Captured from the procedure or modality information associated with the study.</p><p>- Represents modalities such as CT, MR, CR, AR, BI, or other configured modality types.</p><p>- Used to categorize and filter studies by imaging type.</p><p>- Supports workflow routing, modality-based worklists, and reporting.</p>|
|**Languages**|<p>- Indicates the language(s) the patient is fluent in or prefers for communication.</p><p>- Captured as part of the patient’s demographic record during registration or subsequent updates.</p><p>- Used to support appropriate communication, care delivery, and accessibility requirements.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Laterality**|<p>- Specifies the side of the body to which the study or anatomical focus applies.</p><p>- Derived from study-level metadata.</p><p>- Used to ensure correct interpretation, reporting, and clinical context.</p><p>- Common values include bilateral, unilateral left, unilateral right, or unpaired.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Managing Organization**|<p>- Identifies the organization responsible for managing and overseeing the study.</p><p>- Typically derived from the study or account configuration.</p><p>- Used to support ownership, workflow routing, reporting, and administrative control across multi-organization environments.</p><p>- May differ from the imaging or referring organization.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Modality**|<p>- Specifies the imaging modality used to perform the study (for example, CT, MR, US, DX).</p><p>- Values are sourced from the study modality field, typically populated via order entry, modality worklist (MWL), or DICOM metadata.</p><p>- Used to narrow worklists, optimize reading workflows, and segment studies based on imaging technology.</p><p>- Supports multi-select filtering to allow users to view studies performed using one or more modality types.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Modality Modifier Array**|<p>- Represents modifiers applied to the modality or procedure.</p><p>- Captured from order configuration or modality data.</p><p>- Used to refine procedure classification and billing.</p>|
|**Order Custom Field 1**|<p>- An optional, configurable field used to capture additional order-specific information.</p><p>- Data is sourced from the order entry process and is stored with the order record.</p><p>- Can be used for internal tracking, filtering, or reporting purposes.</p><p>- Content is defined by the organization and may vary based on workflow or operational needs.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Order Custom Field 2**|<p>- An optional, configurable field used to capture additional order-specific information.</p><p>- Data is sourced from the order entry process and is stored with the order record.</p><p>- Can be used for internal tracking, filtering, or reporting purposes.</p><p>- Content is defined by the organization and may vary based on workflow or operational needs.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Order Custom Memo**|<p>- An optional free-text field used to capture detailed, order-specific notes or remarks.</p><p>- Information is entered during order creation or update and stored with the order record.</p><p>- Can be used for internal reference, clarification, or workflow-specific communication.</p><p>- Intended for additional context that does not fit into structured order fields.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Order Placement Date/Time**|<p>- Represents the date and time when the order was initially created in the system.</p><p>- Retrieved from the order creation timestamp stored in the order management module.</p><p>- Used for order tracking and lifecycle analysis.</p><p>- Supports filtering by a selected date range; time selection is enabled only after a valid date or date range is chosen.</p><p>- Preset ranges (for example, Today, Last 24 hours, Last 7 days, Last 30 days) can be used for quick filtering.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Order Priority**|<p>- Indicates the urgency level assigned to an order, defining how quickly it should be addressed.</p><p>- Captured during order creation and may be updated by authorized users.</p><p>- Used across clinical, operational, and reporting workflows to support effective triage and order management.</p><p>- Common values include STAT, URGENT, ASAP, ROUTINE, or numeric priorities, depending on system configuration.</p><p>- Influences scheduling precedence, workflow routing, and expected turnaround times.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|<p>**Order of Appropriateness**</p><p></p>|<p>- Represents the appropriateness level assigned to the imaging order.</p><p>- Captured from clinical decision support systems or order entry details.</p><p>- Used to support compliance with imaging guidelines and clinical review.</p>|
|**Order Notes**|<p>- Represents notes associated with the order.</p><p>- Entered during order creation or updated later by authorized users and may also be received from integrated ordering systems.</p><p>- Displays the note text and supports search and filtering based on the entered content.</p><p>- Used to communicate additional instructions or context related to the order for clinical and operational workflows.</p>|
|<p>**Order Status**</p><p></p>|<p>- Represents the current status of the order within the order lifecycle.</p><p>- Automatically assigned and updated by the system or integrated ordering workflows as the order progresses.</p><p>- Displays configurable status values (for example, Created, Pending, or other organizsation-specific statuses) and supports filtering and search.</p><p>- Used to track order progress and support scheduling, processing, and operational workflows.</p>|
|**Patient Address**|<p>- Displays the patient’s recorded address as stored in the patient demographics record.</p><p>- Retrieved from the patient’s demographics.</p><p>- Supports search and multi-select filtering to narrow results based on one or more patient addresses.</p><p>- Includes street-level address values and may contain multiple distinct address entries across patients.</p><p>- A (Blank) option is available to filter records where the patient address is missing or not recorded.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Patient Balance**|<p>- Displays the outstanding financial balance associated with the patient.</p><p>- Represented from billing and financial records.</p><p>- Used for filtering and reviewing studies or orders with pending patient financial responsibility.</p><p>- Represents the amount currently owed by the patient after applied payments, adjustments, or insurance processing.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Patient Cell Phone**|<p>- Displays the patient’s registered mobile phone number.</p><p>- Values are pulled from the patient profile.</p><p>- Used for identifying, filtering, or contacting the patient for notifications such as appointment reminders, order updates, or verification workflows.</p><p>- Values may include country codes and are displayed as stored in the patient profile.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Patient Email**|<p>- Patient’s email ID used during the registration process.</p><p>- Pulled from the Patient Demographics record.</p><p>- Used for electronic communications such as appointment notifications, reports, billing updates, and system alerts, depending on the patient’s contact preferences and consent settings.</p><p>- Captured during patient registration or profile updates.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Patient ID**|<p>- A unique identifier assigned to a patient to consistently identify and track their medical records across the system.</p><p>- Automatically generated by the system during patient registration or received from external systems through HL7/API integrations (such as HIS, EMR, or RIS).</p><p>- Used to associate studies, orders, reports, and all related clinical data with the correct patient.</p><p>- Retrieved from and maintained within the Patient Demographics.</p><p>- Enables accurate retrieval and longitudinal viewing of a patient’s imaging and clinical history.</p><p>- Acts as the primary reference point for patient-level data consistency across workflows and systems.</p><p>- Incorrect, missing, or duplicate values may lead to data fragmentation or incorrect record association.</p><p>- Commonly referred to as the Medical Record Number (MRN) in clinical environments and remains consistent unless formally merged or corrected by authorized users.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Patient Location**|<p>- Indicates the patient’s current or associated location based on visit-level information recorded in the system.</p><p>- Derived from the Visit section and linked to the active or selected visit for the patient.</p><p>- Used to contextualize where the patient is receiving care at the time of the study or order.</p><p>- Determined using visit attributes such as Visit Number and Visit Class (for example, short stay, virtual, or ambulatory).</p><p>- Captured automatically during visit creation or updated through integrations with upstream systems such as HIS or EMR.</p><p>- Supports operational workflows, reporting, and filtering based on care setting or visit type.</p><p>- Ensures studies and orders are accurately associated with the correct visit and care context.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Patient Name**|<p>- Represents the full name of the patient as recorded in the system.</p><p>- Captured at the time of patient registration or intake and maintained within the patient demographic record.</p><p>- Used as a primary identifier for displaying, searching, and validating patient records across workflows.</p><p>- Populated or updated via integrations with external systems such as HIS, EMR, or registration interfaces.</p><p>- Appears on studies, orders, reports, and related clinical and administrative views.</p><p>- Supports accurate patient identification and reduces the risk of record duplication or misassociation.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Patient State/Province**|<p>- Represents the state or province associated with the patient’s address.</p><p>- Captured during patient registration or demographic entry, typically as part of the address details.</p><p>- Used for patient identification, filtering, reporting, and regional or jurisdiction-based workflows.</p><p>- Commonly stored using a standardized two-letter abbreviation (for example, CA for California, ON for Ontario), though values may vary based on data source or integration.</p><p>- Supports administrative, billing, and compliance requirements that depend on geographic location.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Performing Physician**|<p>- Identifies the physician who performs or is responsible for conducting the imaging study.</p><p>- Typically selected from the configured provider directory within the system.</p><p>- Used for clinical accountability, study attribution, and operational tracking.</p><p>- Impacts reporting, physician workload metrics, credentialing compliance, and downstream clinical documentation.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Performing Physician NPI**|<p>- Uniquely identifies the performing physician using their National Provider Identifier (NPI) or internal provider ID.</p><p>- Captured from the physician profile when the performing physician is assigned to the study.</p><p>- Used for billing, claims submission, and regulatory compliance.</p><p>- Sourced from the provider master data and not typically entered manually at the order level.</p><p>- Ensures accurate provider attribution across clinical, financial, and reporting workflows.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Performing Technologist**|<p>- Identifies the technologist who performed or is assigned to perform the imaging study.</p><p>- Captured when the technologist is assigned during scheduling, workflow progression, or exam completion.</p><p>- Used for operational tracking, productivity reporting, and audit purposes.</p><p>- Typically selected from the system’s technologist or user directory.</p><p>- Supports accountability and traceability within clinical and quality workflows.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Pharmaceutical Dosage**|<p>- The amount of a pharmaceutical agent administered to the patient during the imaging procedure.</p><p>- Captured from the modality, technologist input, or integrated clinical systems at the time of administration.</p><p>- Used for audit trails, reporting, quality control, and review of contrast or medication usage across studies.</p><p>- Typically recorded with appropriate units (for example, mg, mL), based on the agent used.</p><p>- Supports clinical accuracy, patient safety, and regulatory documentation requirements.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Pharmaceutical Name**|<p>- The name of the pharmaceutical agent administered during the imaging procedure.</p><p>- Captured from modality data, technologist entry, or integrated clinical systems at the time of administration.</p><p>- Used for reporting, audits, and analysis of pharmaceutical usage across studies.</p><p>- Identifies contrast agents, radiopharmaceuticals, medications, or other clinically relevant substances used in the study.</p><p>- Supports clinical documentation, traceability, and patient safety.</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Pharmaceutical Type**|<p>- The category or classification of the pharmaceutical agent used during the imaging procedure.</p><p>- Captured from technologist input, modality records, or integrated clinical systems when the agent is selected or administered.</p><p>- Used for filtering, reporting, compliance, and clinical review purposes.</p><p>- Indicates the nature of the agent, such as contrast media, radiopharmaceutical, medication, or another procedural agent.</p><p>- Helps standardize documentation and distinguish between different classes of administered substances</p><p>&emsp;</p>|
|**Placer Order #**|<p>- Represents the order number assigned by the ordering (placing) physician or originating clinical system.</p><p>- Captured from the inbound order request (for example, HL7 ORM or electronic requisition).</p><p>- Used for order reconciliation, interoperability, audit tracking, and communication with referring facilities.</p><p>- Serves as an external reference to link the imaging order back to the referring provider’s system.</p><p>- May differ from the internal Filler Order # generated by the imaging application.</p><p>&emsp;.</p>|
