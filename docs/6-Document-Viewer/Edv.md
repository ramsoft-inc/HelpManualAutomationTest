# Embedded Document Viewer (EDV) – Enhanced Integrated Reporting Workflow

The **Embedded Document Viewer (EDV)** introduces a unified and efficient reporting experience by enabling radiologists and physicians to view DICOM images, navigate prior studies, and document findings—all within a single interface in the OmegaAI Image Viewer.
This enhancement supports advanced diagnostic workflows, and complex reporting needs while ensuring accuracy, traceability, and ease of use.

The EDV replaces the older document viewer experience with a modern, flexible, and fully integrated panel that works in harmony with the Image Viewer.

## 1. Accessing the Embedded Document Viewer 

To open the EDV:

1. Sign in to **OmegaAI**.
1. Go to the **Worklist**.
1. Select the desired study.
1. Right-click to open the **Omega Dial** → choose **Image Viewer**.
1. Inside the Image Viewer:
   1. **Study Explorer** appears on the left (All / Current / Prior).
   1. **DICOM images** open in the central canvas.
1. Click the **EDV icon** on the top-right toolbar to open the document viewer beside the images.

The EDV panel will slide in, displaying the study report (Preliminary or Final) in a collapsible, navigable format.

## 2. Key Enhancements in the New EDV 

The redesigned EDV offers multiple workflow advantages: 

### Fully Embedded in the Image Viewer 

- Reports open **next to the DICOM images**.
- Eliminates previous navigation steps.
- Supports side-by-side comparison without switching modules.
  
### Collapsible EDV Navigation Panel 

- Unified panel with:
  - All studies (current, prior, related)
  - Preliminary and final reports
  - Document explorer and metadata
- Allows smooth toggling across reports while viewing images.
  
### Dedicated Toolbars for Report Editing 

Each viewer now has its own toolbar:

- **Image Viewer Toolbar:** zoom, windowing, MPR, measurements
- **Document Viewer Toolbar:** edit, sign, amend, annotate
  
### Expanded Editing Tools

- Text formatting (bold/italic/underline/strikethrough)
- Alignment & indentation
- Numbered/bulleted lists
- Header formatting
- Insert signature
- Template editing
- Bookmarking & structured sections
  
### Version History Enhancements 

- Complete timeline of edits
- Includes editor name, date, timestamp
- **“Check”** button highlights the exact edited sections
- **“Done”** confirms the review
- Optional **Edit** button per version
- Ability to exit via “X” icon and reopen via 3-dot menu

### Peer Review Workflow 

Accessible from the 3-dot menu:

- Standardized scoring options
- Comment editing with full formatting controls
- Submission tracking
  
  ### Integrated Technologist Forms 
  
- Integrated Technologist Forms in the DV’s top-left corner let users review data, submit the form, or cancel and return to the report.
  
### AI Transcription Assistance 

- Detects sections of the final report
- Helps generate structured summaries
- Refer to “AI Transcription” in the DV Help Manual for details 
  
### Document Actions Panel 

At the bottom of the EDV:

- View pages
- Print
- Download
- Zoom in/out
- Horizontal scroll for wide reports

### Measurement Panel Relocated 

- Now on the left of DV for better visibility
- Closer alignment with Image Viewer tools


## 3. Working with Final Reports

#### *Steps:* 

Open the study → open EDV → choose **Final Report** → Amend/Share/Highlight Critical Finding.

#### *Tools available:* 

- ***Amend Report***
  - Use this feature to update or correct final reports.
- ***Share***
      - Share the report or send the study. (See Study/Report Share in the OmegaAI section under Advanced Topics.) 
- ***Technologist Form***
      - A study-level form that must be completed before viewing images in the Image Viewer. The template is provided by the imaging organisation associated with the study.
- ***3-Dot Menu***
  Access additional tools:
   - **Critical Finding Toggle**
     Enable to highlight critical findings in reports; disable when not required.
   - **Version History**
     1. View event logs and manage edits: 
     2. Check – Highlight edited areas
     3. Done – Close review
     4. Edit – Modify a specific version
     5. X – Exit the viewer
   
## 4. Working with Preliminary Reports 

**Toolbar Options**

- Done
- Done & Open Next
- Voice Dictation (Augnito)
- 3-Dot Menu:
  - Critical Finding toggle
  - Bookmarks (with picklists, fields, and categories)
  - Version History

**Editing Tools Available**

- All formatting options
- Ability to edit template
- Signature support
- Finalization options

**Printing**

- Preliminary reports support printing from EDV.

## 5. Sharing Reports 

- Select the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the Document Viewer. 

- Choose whether to **share the report** or **send the study** using the available options. For detailed instructions, see Study/Report Share in the Advanced Topics section of the OmegaAI Help Manual.  https://help.omegaai.com/docs/Advanced-Topics/Sharefunctionui
